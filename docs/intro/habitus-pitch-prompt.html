Habitus — Pitch Deck

An AI real-estate agent — a “digital urbanist-detective”. Full slide-by-slide content brief. Language of the deck: English.

HOW TO USE THIS DOCUMENT. This is a content brief for generating a 13-slide pitch deck. Apply our existing design system (colors, fonts, logo, layout grid) — do not invent a new visual style. Keep the tone confident, concrete and technical; investors are the audience. Write every slide in English. Prefer short, punchy on-slide copy (headline + 3–5 bullets max); put the longer explanations into the speaker notes. Highlight the three product advantages — Convenient · Precise · Honest — wherever they fit. Fields per slide: Headline (big on-slide title), On-slide (bullet copy), Visual (what to show), Speaker notes (what the presenter says).

Global messaging — the spine of the pitch

One-liner: Habitus turns a plain-language wish into a ranked shortlist of real apartments, each with a lifestyle dossier — grounded in real city data, never invented.

The problem in one sentence: People buy a way of life, but every listing portal only lets them filter square meters.

Why we win (repeat this trio everywhere):

Convenient — one conversation in natural language replaces a dozen browser tabs and manual filters.
Precise — we rank by real life: family logistics, social surroundings, sunlight, noise and the view — not just price and size.
Honest — every claim is grounded in database facts; if data is missing, the block degrades — we never invent a number or hallucinate.

Golden thread of the pitch: “A home is a 10–15 year, multi-million decision made blind today. Habitus makes it informed.”
